First-time setup on this iPad

Each wearable must be granted to this page once, from this browser. Pairing them to the iPad is not enough — that is a Bluetooth bond between iPad and wearable, while this is a permission the browser holds for this page. Grant it once and every session afterwards connects on its own.

Do this with the wearables on and not yet in a game. Register one side at a time and pick the unit you are holding; the page checks that the board agrees before accepting it.
